The argument between Sam Neill and Kristin Scott Thomas in The Horse Whisperer was recorded with both actors miked onto separate tracks, which left the overlapping dialogue available afterwards as independent material rather than as one fixed performance. The account given here is of removing more of the air between the lines and tightening those overlaps deliberately, so that the exchange holds no respite in it and a listener cannot draw a breath anywhere inside the scene. The same speaker describes working with Per Hallberg and with Ridley Scott on Black Hawk Down, where the helicopters entering Mogadishu were treated as something closer to a science fiction ballet, people landing on a different planet. That decision ran the other way, holding back almost all of the helicopter sound in favour of music, so the sequence plays from inside a single point of view.

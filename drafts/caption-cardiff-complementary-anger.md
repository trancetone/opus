Jack Cardiff, lighting the sequence in Black Narcissus where Sister Ruth attempts to kill Deborah Kerr at the bell, put green filters into the fill light and pinkish colours into the sun effects, which is a complementary pair rather than a naturalistic one. His reference was a Van Gogh painted in Arles, the café with the huge billiard table, where the green of the cloth sits against the reddish lamps ranged around it, and what he says he wanted out of that combination was anger. The sequence carries a second constraint that is easy to miss, because it was designed and shot to an exact piece of music that Brian Easdale had already composed, which means the cutting pattern existed before any of the photography did. Watching those minutes with both facts in hand changes what Powell and Cardiff appear to be doing in them.
